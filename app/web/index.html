The 4 Alternative Financial Pillars

We evaluate disciplined cash-flow behaviors that traditional credit bureaus completely ignore.

Rent Regularity

Proof of steady housing payments through UPI, bank transfers, or verifiable receipts serves as a powerful proxy for repayment reliability.

Utility Discipline

Continuous, on-time electricity, water, and broadband bill payments demonstrate routine household financial discipline.

Mobile Connectivity

Account tenure and steady airtime recharge frequency indicate lifestyle stability and digital payment engagement.

Cash-Flow Stability

Weekly gig platform deposits, daily merchant sales, and positive balance days confirm real debt-servicing capacity.
